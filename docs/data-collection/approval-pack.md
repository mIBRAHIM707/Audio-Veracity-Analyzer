## One-page review brief

**Purpose.** AVA is a student research prototype for detecting synthetic Urdu and
Pakistani Punjabi speech. Participants would provide genuine recordings; only
people who separately agree to cloning may have artificial versions of their
voice made for the approved research. AVA does not identify people or authenticate
bank customers. Research results are not assessments of a participant's honesty.

**Requested first step.** Review a proposed pilot of **2–4 adults**, subject to
institutional confirmation of adult eligibility and the approved recruitment
method. Review the pilot's consent process, recording quality and data handling
before authorizing expansion. The team's first-month planning proposal is 5–8
participants per team member, 20–32 total; the canonical long-term target remains
60–80 speakers, mostly Majhi Punjabi with other regions represented and roughly
equal participation by women and men. These are planning targets, not permission
to pressure participants or expand the approved sample.

**Procedure.** After questions and signed consent, assign a coded speaker ID and
collect the approved minimal demographic form. Record 10–12 minutes: (A) 20–30
read sentences in Shahmukhi Punjabi and Urdu, including some English words;
(B) free speech using safe everyday prompts; and (C) a fictional blocked-bank-card
call. Use an approved Asterisk lab call or a web recorder that saves WAV. No
WhatsApp voice notes. Participants can skip a prompt, pause or stop. Consent and
questions occur before recording; do not rush them into the 10–12-minute window.

**Language coverage.** D6 targets an overall Urdu/Punjabi balance of 50:50 across
the corpus. FR-D5 requires at least 15% code-switched clips in each genuine and
synthetic partition. Code-switching overlaps the language categories; it is not
a third share to add to 100%. FR-D4 does not prescribe per-session percentages.
The supervisor must approve the session prompt allocation before use.

**Risks and controls.** A voice may be recognizable despite replacing names with
IDs. A cloned voice could be misused if exposed. Fictional scripts avoid real
bank details and third-party information. Use restricted university-controlled
storage in Pakistan or an approved encrypted drive with one backup; keep consent
and the identity-to-ID link separately. Access, deletion dates, backup handling
and any off-site processing must be specified before consent. There is no default
public release of voice audio, speaker-level manifests or trained models.

**Optional later activities.** Cloning requires its own consent choice and approved
protocol. Recording-only participants contribute genuine audio to the test split
only under FR-D11. The later FR-S1 listening study proposes at least 30 Pakistani
listeners, 40 balanced clips held apart from training speakers, and three
conditions: listeners alone, AVA alone, and listeners shown AVA's score. It needs
an approved protocol and listener information/consent; this recording pilot does
not start it or authorize use of someone's voice as a listening stimulus.

**Decision requested.** Identify the approving body, review the proposed pilot and
each optional activity, complete the policy fields below, and record the approval
reference and any restrictions. Supervisor review is not institutional approval
unless the designated body expressly provides for that route.

## Participant information — plain-language draft

**Please do not use this sheet until its blanks and translations are approved.**

We are studying computer-made speech in Urdu and Pakistani Punjabi. We would
like you to read some sentences, talk about ordinary topics and act out a made-up
bank call. The recording takes about 10–12 minutes. We will explain the study and
answer your questions before we record anything.

Joining is your choice. Saying no, skipping a question or stopping will not affect
your grades or your relationship with the university or project team. You do not
need to give a reason. You can ask for a break. We will explain any payment and
what happens if you stop before you decide: **[approved payment terms required]**.

Please use only invented details in the role-play. Do not say a real name, account
number, card number, PIN, password, one-time code, address or telephone number.
Do not tell us another person's private information. This is not a real bank call.

With your recording permission, we may cut the recording into short clips and
make approved research versions that sound like telephone audio. If you also
choose voice cloning, we may make artificial speech that sounds like you. This
artificial speech is not something you actually said. We will not clone your voice
if you say no to that choice. Under the current project plan, recordings without
cloning permission may be used only for genuine-speech testing, not training.

We give your files a code instead of your name, but someone who knows you might
still recognize your voice. The people allowed to access or hear your recordings
are **[approved recipients and purposes required]**. We keep recordings at
**[approved storage description required]** until **[deletion date required]**.
We keep the signed form and the link to your code separately. The approved backup
and deletion arrangements are **[required]**. We cannot promise that a recognizable
voice becomes anonymous simply by removing its name.

We will not put your voice or speaker-level records on a public website under
this pilot. We will not upload them to an outside service unless that specific
processing has been approved, explained and separately agreed to. Sharing with
examiners or using a clip in a listening study also requires the applicable
permission. You may decline optional choices independently.

To withdraw, contact **[approved contact required]** and give your reference ID
**[issued privately after consent]**. We will stop using your recordings and
trace and delete your clips and the artificial voices made from them under the
approved withdrawal procedure. This includes working copies and backups. If your
audio has been used to train a model, the approved procedure for stopping use,
deleting or rebuilding that model is **[required before enrollment]**. The expected
response and deletion times are **[required]**. Do not ask participants to rely
on an untested claim that their contribution can simply be erased from weights.

For questions contact **[required]**. To raise a concern outside the project team,
contact **[independent institutional contact required]**. You will receive a copy
of the approved information, your choices and your reference ID.

the withdrawn data. Confirm completion to the participant through the approved

remediation method must be resolved before collecting voices.

## Recording-day checklist — use only after the start gate passes

- [ ] Confirm the valid approval reference, approved protocol/form versions,
  operator and participant eligibility. Obtain signed choices before recording.
- [ ] Explain the voluntary choices, payment, withdrawal reference and stop signal.
  Give the participant a copy. Record permissions in the restricted inventory.
- [ ] Use only a coded ID in filenames and logs. Keep age band, gender, home
  district, dialect, languages and phone model in the approved short demographic
  form; collect no exact birth date, national ID or real banking details.
- [ ] Test the approved capture route using non-participant test material first.
  Use the Asterisk lab call or WAV-saving web recorder; no WhatsApp, personal cloud
  sync or unapproved upload. Confirm no bystander or real customer is recorded.
- [ ] Record the approved device, capture sample rate, channel count, bit depth and
  call codec where applicable. FR-D4 specifies the route/WAV capture, not a universal
  microphone sample rate. Do not invent one or silently replace the native capture.
- [ ] Follow the reviewed read/free-speech/fictional-bank prompts within the planned
  10–12 minutes. Pause or stop when asked. If private details are spoken, stop and
  follow the approved deletion/re-recording procedure; do not propagate that take.
- [ ] Preserve each accepted native original unchanged with an integrity hash;
  produce normalized/resampled 3–6-second clips only as separately identified
  derivatives. Recording is not an automatic grant of cloning or training rights.
- [ ] Transfer to the approved external controlled/encrypted storage and its one
  approved backup. Verify hashes and access. Clear temporary recorder copies under
  the approved policy after verified transfer; keep consent records separate.
- [ ] Complete QA and inventory/permission checks. Keep all voices, signed forms,
  identity links, personal manifests and trained-on-consented-voice checkpoints
  outside Git. Check repository guards without importing private files into tests.

## Readiness QA and explicit start gate

The draft is ready for human review when its source mapping is checked, every
missing policy has a visible required field, separate choices are intelligible,
and no passage implies approval or public release. Completing the draft is a
preparatory milestone; it does not complete approval or collection.

**Recording start gate — all must be evidenced in the controlled project records:**

- [ ] The designated institution has approved the exact pilot and recorded its
  reference, scope, validity and conditions; supervisor review alone is not assumed
  sufficient. The datasheet references that approval (FR-E1).
- [ ] Core policy fields, contact routes, retention/deletion dates, compensation
  and restrictions are complete; participant-facing forms contain no placeholders.
- [ ] Recruitment, adult eligibility, prompt allocation and translations have been
  reviewed. Optional cloning/off-site/examiner/stimulus permissions are unbundled.
- [ ] Storage, separate consent records, access controls, backup and stop behavior
  have been checked using fictional material. A fictional reference ID can be
  traced through derivatives and deletion, including backup/model-handling steps.
- [ ] Tools and uses have passed the licence register check. No public release or
  off-site use can proceed on a general research checkbox alone (FR-E3–FR-E5).
- [ ] Each participant's signed choices are verified before their recording. The
  collector and an independent checker record the gate result outside Git.

**Separate later gates:** no cloning until the protocol covers it and that person
has explicitly opted in; no listening study until its protocol, listener consent,
voice-stimulus permissions and speaker-disjoint set are ready; no expansion until
the pilot review and institutional scope allow it. Any failed or unknown gate
means the relevant activity stays stopped. Current status (4 October 2026):
